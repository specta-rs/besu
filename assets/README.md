## Notes

- The wordmark is Space Mono Bold, outlined in the SVGs.
- Keep clear space equal to one grid cell on all sides.
- Minimum lockup width: 240 px. Minimum mark size: 24 px.
- The favicon is the dark tile; at 16 px the structure drops away and the live column plus outlier remain.
